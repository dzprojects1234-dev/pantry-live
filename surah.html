🌙 Sebelum Solat Fardhu Before Obligatory Prayer
Subuh (Fajr) Fajr (Dawn)
  • Surah Al-Ikhlas & Al-Kafirun Rasulullah SAW membaca kedua surah ini dalam solat sunat dua rakaat sebelum Subuh. (Sahih Muslim, no. 726) The Prophet ﷺ recited these two surahs in the two rak'ahs of Sunnah before Fajr. (Sahih Muslim, no. 726)
Zohor & Subuh Dhuhr & Fajr
  • Surah Al-Hujurat hingga Al-Buruj Sunat membaca surah-surah panjang (Mufassal) dalam solat fardhu Zohor dan Subuh bagi yang tidak bermusafir. (Amalan masyhur dalam kitab fiqh; lihat IslamQA) It is Sunnah to recite from the longer Mufassal surahs in Dhuhr and Fajr for non-travellers. (Well-known practice in fiqh; see IslamQA)
Asar & Isyak Asr & Isha
  • Surah At-Tariq hingga Al-Bayyinah Sunat membaca surah-surah pertengahan (Awsaat al-Mufassal) dalam solat Asar dan Isyak. (Amalan masyhur; lihat IslamQA) It is Sunnah to recite from the middle Mufassal surahs in Asr and Isha. (Well-known practice; see IslamQA)
Maghrib Maghrib
  • Surah Az-Zalzalah hingga An-Nas Sunat membaca surah-surah pendek (Qisaar al-Mufassal) dalam solat Maghrib. (Amalan masyhur; lihat IslamQA) It is Sunnah to recite from the shorter Mufassal surahs in Maghrib. (Well-known practice; see IslamQA)
💡 Nota: Susunan ini berdasarkan amalan sunat dalam kitab fiqh. Ia bukan kewajipan, tetapi galakan untuk menghidupkan sunnah.
💡 Note: This arrangement is based on Sunnah practices in fiqh books. It is not obligatory but encouraged to revive the Sunnah.
✨ Selepas Solat Fardhu After Obligatory Prayer
Semua Solat 5 Waktu All Five Daily Prayers
  • Ayat Al-Kursi Sesiapa membacanya selepas setiap solat fardhu, tidak ada yang menghalangnya masuk syurga melainkan kematian. (Riwayat al-Tabarani; dinilai sahih oleh sebahagian ulama) Whoever recites it after every obligatory prayer, nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death. (Narrated by al-Tabarani; graded authentic by some scholars)
  • Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas Digalakkan membaca Al-Mu‘awwidzat (tiga surah terakhir) selepas solat. (Sunan Abi Dawud, no. 1518; Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 3575) It is recommended to recite the Mu'awwidhat (last three surahs) after prayer. (Sunan Abi Dawud, no. 1518; Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 3575)
Subuh Fajr
  • Surah Yasin Amalan membaca Surah Yasin pada waktu pagi. (Sunan al-Darimi, no. 3418; sanadnya hasan menurut sebahagian ulama) The practice of reciting Surah Yasin in the morning. (Sunan al-Darimi, no. 3418; graded hasan by some scholars)
Maghrib Maghrib
  • Surah Al-Waqi‘ah Sesiapa membacanya setiap malam, dia tidak akan ditimpa kefakiran. (Riwayat Abu Ya'la & Ibnu Asakir; dinilai hasan oleh sebahagian ulama) Whoever recites it every night will not be afflicted by poverty. (Narrated by Abu Ya'la & Ibn Asakir; graded hasan by some scholars)
Isyak Isha
  • Surah Al-Mulk Nabi SAW tidak tidur sehingga membaca Surah Al-Mulk. (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 2891; dinilai sahih oleh al-Albani) The Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he recited Surah Al-Mulk. (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 2891; graded authentic by al-Albani)
🕌 Amalan Hari Jumaat Friday Practices
Solat Subuh Jumaat Friday Fajr
  • Surah As-Sajdah & Al-Insan Nabi SAW sering membaca kedua surah ini dalam solat Subuh hari Jumaat. (Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 891; Sahih Muslim, no. 879) The Prophet ﷺ often recited these two surahs in Friday Fajr prayer. (Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 891; Sahih Muslim, no. 879)
Solat Jumaat Friday Prayer (Jumu'ah)
  • Surah Al-Jumu‘ah & Al-Munafiqun Nabi SAW membaca Surah Al-Jumu‘ah pada rakaat pertama dan Al-Munafiqun pada rakaat kedua. (Sahih Muslim, no. 877) The Prophet ﷺ recited Surah Al-Jumu'ah in the first rak'ah and Al-Munafiqun in the second. (Sahih Muslim, no. 877)
Sepanjang Hari Jumaat Throughout Friday
  • Surah Al-Kahfi Sesiapa membacanya pada hari Jumaat, akan diterangi cahaya di antara dua Jumaat. (Riwayat al-Hakim, no. 3392 & al-Baihaqi, no. 5996; dinilai sahih oleh al-Albani) Whoever recites it on Friday will be illuminated with light between the two Fridays. (Narrated by al-Hakim, no. 3392 & al-Bayhaqi, no. 5996; graded authentic by al-Albani)
🌟 Hari & Malam Istimewa Special Days & Nights
Bulan Ramadhan Ramadan
  • Surah Al-Qadr Sangat digalakkan membaca dan merenung maknanya, terutamanya pada 10 malam terakhir. (Amalan yang masyhur; lihat riwayat tentang Lailatul Qadr dalam Sahih al-Bukhari) Highly recommended to recite and reflect on its meaning, especially in the last 10 nights. (Well-known practice; see narrations on Laylat al-Qadr in Sahih al-Bukhari)
Malam Hari Night Time
  • Surah As-Sajdah & Al-Mulk Nabi SAW tidak tidur sehingga membaca kedua-dua surah ini. (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 2892; dinilai sahih oleh al-Albani) The Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he recited these two surahs. (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, no. 2892; graded authentic by al-Albani)
💡 Nota: Amalan pada hari-hari istimewa ini adalah galakan untuk memperbanyakkan bacaan Al-Quran, bukan terhad kepada surah-surah yang disenaraikan sahaja.
💡 Note: Practices on these special days encourage increasing Qur'anic recitation, not limited to the surahs listed.
